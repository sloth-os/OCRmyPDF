About

      

        This is OCRmyPDF
        compiled to WebAssembly (via
        Pyodide), with
        tesseract-wasm
        for recognition. Everything runs on your device. Install it (from your
        browser's install/menu) to work offline.
      

      

        On desktop Chromium browsers this app registers as a
        file handler for PDFs — after installing it, you can
        right-click a PDF → Open with → OCRmyPDF.
